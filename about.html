Founder & Vision

Our Story

Savanna Brew Café opened in 2019 with one small espresso machine and a big dream: to celebrate Kenyan coffee. Founder Amina Otieno grew up on her grandparents' coffee farm in Nyeri and wanted to bring that flavour to the city. Today the café supports local farmers, uses eco-friendly packaging and hosts community events every month.

"Every harvest in Nyeri carries the dedication of our smallholder farmers. When we pour a cup in Westlands, we are sharing our family heritage with every guest." — Mrs. Amina Otieno, Founder of Savanna Brew Café

What Drives Us

Our Values

Every cup and pastry we serve is anchored in three foundational commitments.

  • Quality Quality: only freshly roasted, single-origin Kenyan beans.
  • Community Community: fair prices for farmers and a welcoming space for all.
  • Sustainability Sustainability: compostable cups and zero-waste pastries.
